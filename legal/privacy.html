Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Welcome to SkinCore ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, and profile photo when you register.
  • Skin Profile: Skin type and preferences you voluntarily provide.
  • Usage Data: Products you scan, search history, favorites, and routines you create.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and push notification token (FCM token) for notifications.
  • Photos: Images you upload for product scanning or routine posts. These are stored securely and used only for the intended feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the SkinCore app and its features.
  • To personalize your experience based on your skin type and preferences.
  • To send push notifications about activity related to your account (likes, comments).
  • To analyze product ingredients and provide safety scores.
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob (non-premium users only).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, push notifications, and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements to non-premium users.
  • Apple App Store: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Cloud Storage Providers: For storing uploaded images securely.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@skincore.beauty.

5. Children's Privacy

SkinCore is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and secure token-based authentication to protect your information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@skincore.beauty.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile app does not use cookies. We may use analytics tools to understand usage patterns and improve the app experience.

9. Third-Party Links

SkinCore may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of SkinCore after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
privacy@skincore.beauty
